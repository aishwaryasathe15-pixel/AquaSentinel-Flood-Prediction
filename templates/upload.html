Required columns for retraining

The target column is necessary to teach the model which risk occurred in each historical row.

rainfall_mm
water_level_m
humidity_pct
temperature_c
elevation_m
previous_flood
flood_risk

Target labels: Low, Medium, High, Extreme. At least 20 valid rows and 2 categories are required. Without flood_risk, the CSV is saved for visualization only.
